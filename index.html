A run is saved when it finishes, or when you stop it after at least 8 scored notes. "Best" and "Fastest at 90%+" count Play along runs only.

Mixer

Changes how loud each drum sounds from this page: demo, backing patterns, sequencer preview and your pad sounds.
DrumVolumeRing (how long it sounds)
Volume 0% mutes a drum. Ring can shorten a cymbal's tail or let it ring up to the full length of its recording (longer than that has no further effect). Hit Test to hear a drum at its current settings.

Sequencer

Tempo follows the main Tempo slider.
To record: pick the bars and grid, press ● Record my playing, wait out the 4-beat count-in, then play. The pattern loops and each pass is layered on the last, so you can build a groove in layers. Press Stop (or Space) when done. Hits snap to the nearest step.
Click or tap squares to place hits (drag across a row with the mouse to paint). An open hi-hat replaces the closed one on the same step.

Pad mapping

If a pad lights the wrong lane or nothing, press Learn on that lane, then hit the pad. Keyboard fallback, left to right: A S D F J K L ;  ·  open hi-hat: G  ·  hollow block = open hi-hat  ·
